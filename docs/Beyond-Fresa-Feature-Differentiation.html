1. Headline differentiators

These are the capabilities that most clearly separate KingFisher Wings from a classic Fresa-style staff ERP with a thin customer view.

01 · Commercial
Quote negotiation & counter-offers

Customer can accept, reject, or counter-offer. Staff negotiate with revise-and-send, accept/reject counter, and a full negotiation timeline. Same pattern mirrored for vendor cost offers.

02 · Customer Portal
Messages & invoice disputes

Party-shared message threads with attachments and staff inbox/replies. Structured invoice disputes with review/resolve on the staff side — not just email.

03 · Finance self-serve
Invoices, payment proofs & online pay

Portal invoices with PDF, attachments, remittance/payment-proof upload, Stripe checkout and payment status. Credit summary, open items, and statement-style exports.

04 · Delivery proof
Formal delivery acknowledgement

Customer submits goods-receipt acknowledgement with signature upload — closes the last-mile loop without WhatsApp chasing.

05 · Status flexibility
Custom status catalog buttons

Tenant-defined status labels/catalogs for quotations, shipments, and jobs — add custom status options without hard-coding every label in the UI.

06 · Booking without premature jobs
Quotation-scoped booking forms

Customer fills and submits booking forms (all modes + docs) on the quotation. Admin sees the form only after submit; job is created only on Generate Job.

07 · Visibility
Track-by-anything + public track

Lookup by job, shipment, container, BL/AWB, barcode, token. Portal milestones + public track without login. Typed document visibility (staff / customer / vendor).

08 · Vendor depth
Full vendor portal (not pay-only)

Vendor auth, job offers, cost negotiation, documents, costing (cost-only), finance, payouts, payment proofs, and ops continuum attachments — a true second portal.

3. Admin / Staff ERP — extras beyond Fresa

Staff retain Fresa-style job/shipment/quote shells (detail tabs, path hints, costing, org, routing). The items below are the meaningful additions or deepenings.

3.1 Commercial & booking

  • BEYOND FRESA Negotiation desk — timeline, accept/reject customer counter, revise-and-send.
  • BEYOND FRESA Booking form gate — admin sees submitted customer form only; Generate Job materializes form onto the job.
  • BEYOND FRESA Mode booking forms — Sea FCL/LCL, land, road, courier, customs clearance, warehouse, air compliance with mandatory docs.
  • BEYOND FRESA Verify → Approve → Generate Shipment / Job — explicit Fresa-like gates, plus no silent provisional job from form open.
  • BEYOND FRESA Pass-to-vendor / job offers from quotation or job into vendor negotiation.
  • FRESA-ALIGNED Quotation/shipment/job detail tabs with path_hints and action flags for FE.

3.2 Status catalogs & workflow polish

  • BEYOND FRESA Custom status catalog APIs for quotations, shipments, jobs — create/update/deactivate label options; status-options endpoints for UI buttons.
  • BEYOND FRESA Staff change-status with catalog codes mapped to system statuses.
  • BEYOND FRESA Staff portal inbox for messages & dispute management (CCP staff APIs).

3.3 Ops, track, docs & continuum

  • BEYOND FRESA Public / staff Track & Trace — track-by-anything; unified milestone history.
  • BEYOND FRESA Typed document uploads with visibility to customer/vendor; booking-form document kinds.
  • BEYOND FRESA Ops continuum comments & attachments across quotation / shipment / job for staff + portals.
  • BEYOND FRESA Delivery acknowledgement admin view of customer POD/receipt.
  • BEYOND FRESA Exception / KPI / job-not-invoiced-DO / job-finance-by-shipment-date packs (demo “beat Fresa” reports).
  • BEYOND FRESA Closed-job permission-aware overrides; SABER / RA / ISF refs; overseas agent org role.
  • BEYOND FRESA Favourites, staff activity emails, notifications emitter across modules.

3.4 Masters & platform

  • BEYOND FRESA Multi-tenant hierarchy: SuperAdmin → Company → Tenant → Tenant Admin → staff roles.
  • BEYOND FRESA Portal/vendor user administration from party masters; permission packs.
  • FRESA-ALIGNED Deep masters (ports, charges, tariffs, zip distances, freight specs) feeding quotes and jobs.

6. Demo talking points (use these)

  1. “Negotiate in-product.” Customer counters a quote; staff revises; vendor counters a cost offer — one commercial language on both sides.
  2. “Portal is not a track-only site.” Messages, disputes, invoices, payment proofs, Stripe, credit requests, booking forms, acknowledgement.
  3. “No fake jobs.” Opening a booking form does not invent a job; admin Generate Job is the cutover; form appears for staff after customer submit.
  4. “Statuses you own.” Custom status catalog buttons for quotes/shipments/jobs — tenant vocabulary, not only hard-coded enums.
  5. “Three audiences, one ops spine.” Staff ERP + customer portal + vendor portal share track, typed docs, and continuum attachments with the right ACLs.
  6. “Beat-Fresa ops packs.” Exceptions, DO-not-invoiced, shipment-date finance, closed overrides, overseas agent / compliance refs.
