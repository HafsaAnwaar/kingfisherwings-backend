---

## 1. What buyers need before they trust freight software

| Need | How Kingfisher can demonstrate it | Status for marketing |
|------|-----------------------------------|----------------------|
| Product capabilities | Live demo of quote → job → invoice → portal → track | Ready (product exists) |
| Data protection | Security page using only verified claims (`03`) | Ready to draft |
| Business privacy | Tenant isolation + portal ownership explanation | Ready |
| Operational transparency | Continuum diagrams + department workflows | Ready |
| Ease of use | Guided demo scripts; sanitized screenshots | Needs FE screenshot pack |
| Support availability | Published support hours/channels | **Needs company facts** |
| Product demonstrations | Bookable demo CTA | Process decision |

| Business continuity | Backup restore evidence | **Do not advertise until verified** |

## 2. Trust elements to show on the website

### Allowed now (with honest wording)
- Clear explanation of who can access what (staff / customer / vendor / admin)  
- Security & privacy page (verified protections only)  
- Workflow diagrams from real product stages  
- Product screenshots with dummy data  
- Payment provider clarification (cards handled by provider)  
- FAQ addressing mobile/WhatsApp/accounting scope honestly  
- Demo and contact paths  

### Not allowed until real
- Customer logos  
- Testimonials  
- Case study metrics  
- Awards  
- SOC/ISO badges  
- “Trusted by X,000 forwarders” counts  
- Uptime % SLAs  
- Invented office maps or partner networks  

---

## 3. Conversion design recommendations

1. **Primary CTA everywhere:** Request a demonstration.  
2. **Secondary CTA:** Review Security & Privacy.  
3. **Demo script:** 25–30 minutes covering continuum + portals + one finance post + track privacy.  
4. **Objection handling:** CargoWise “too heavy”; spreadsheets “too risky”; GoFreight “billing-only worry” → use `04`.  
5. **Proof artifacts:** Sandbox tenant walkthrough; PDF security overview already in `docs/client-review/`.  
6. **Post-demo:** Tenant pilot plan (modules enabled, users invited, sample parties).

## 5. Credibility language examples

**Good:** “Online payments are processed by a payment provider. Kingfisher records the receipt in your company ledger.”  
**Bad:** “Bank-grade vault stores your card data.”

**Good:** “Each company works in a separated business space with role-based staff access.”  
**Bad:** “Military-grade isolation guarantees zero breaches.”

**Good:** “Public tracking shares shipment progress without exposing commercial costs.”  
**Bad:** “AI-powered zero-trust tracking matrix.”
