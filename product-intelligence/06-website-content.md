**Tone:** Professional, premium, confident, clear, enterprise — never hyperbolic.  
**Rule:** Every claim maps to implemented capabilities in `02` and verified security in `03`.

## 4. Platform introduction

Kingfisher Technologies is a multi-tenant freight management platform for logistics companies that need more than a simple tracking page. Teams create quotations, convert them into bookings and jobs, collect documents, advance operational milestones, invoice customers, settle vendors, and give customers and vendors controlled portal access — while public tracking shares progress without exposing confidential commercial detail.

## 6. Security messaging (approved)

- Separate secure sign-in for staff, customers, and vendors.  
- Role-based permissions inside each company.  
- Business information kept separate from other companies on the platform.  
- Private document access for authorized users.  
- Public tracking without exposing confidential costs or internal notes.  
- Online payments processed by a payment provider; Kingfisher updates invoices and receipts in your books.  
- Session controls and activity accountability for business oversight.

**Avoid:** “Unbreakable,” fake certifications, card vault claims.

---

## 7. Customer portal messaging

Give your customers a controlled window into their business with you: quotations, shipment progress, documents they are allowed to see, invoices, notifications, and payment options — without giving them access to your internal costing or other customers’ data.

---

## 8. Vendor portal messaging

Work with carriers, agents, and service vendors in a dedicated portal. Vendors can view assigned work, exchange documents, submit purchase invoices for review, and follow AP-related information — while your team remains in control of posting and financial approval.

---

## 9. Freight operations messaging

Kingfisher supports the modes forwarders actually run — air import and export, ocean FCL and LCL, inland and courier movements, NVOCC consolidation workflows, and warehouse-linked jobs — with booking forms, containers, milestones, and documentation living on the operational file.

---

## 10. Customs clearance messaging

Customs clearance is managed as a structured job with clear stages from quotation and acceptance through document collection, classification, filing, assessment, duty payment, clearance, release, and invoicing — so Sales, Operations, and Accounts know their responsibilities on every file.

---

## 11. Financial management messaging

Commercial work should not end at delivery. Kingfisher connects job charges to customer and vendor invoices, posts into the general ledger, supports receipts and payments with allocations, and presents AR/AP statements and management reports for clearer financial control.

## 15. Frequently asked questions

**What is Kingfisher Technologies?**  
A freight and logistics management platform for forwarders and logistics companies covering quotations, multi-mode jobs, documents, portals, and financial control.

**Who is it for?**  
Freight forwarders, NVOCCs, customs-focused operators, and logistics companies that need connected operations and accounts.

**Does it support air and sea?**  
Yes — air import/export, ocean FCL/LCL, plus inland, courier, NVOCC, warehouse, and customs clearance job types.

**Is there a customer portal?**  
Yes. Customers can work with quotations, shipments, documents, invoices, and notifications according to permissions you grant.

**Is there a vendor portal?**  
Yes. Vendors can access assigned work and related documents and submit purchase invoices for your team to review.

**Does Kingfisher replace my accounting system?**  
Kingfisher includes general ledger, AR/AP, vouchers, and financial reports. Some companies still integrate with external accounting — discuss fit during demo.

**How do online payments work?**  
Online checkout is processed by a payment provider. Kingfisher records the receipt against invoices in your company books.

**How is my company data separated?**  
Each company operates in its own tenant space with authenticated access and role-based permissions.

**Can customers track shipments without logging in?**  
Public track & trace supports lookup by reference, with sensitive commercial details withheld.

**Do you have a mobile app?**  
A native mobile app is not positioned as a current product offering from this platform investigation. Browser access is the primary model.

**Is WhatsApp built in as the main channel?**  
Email and in-app notifications are the primary channels. WhatsApp should not be marketed as a core completed channel.
